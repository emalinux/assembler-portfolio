## 🛠️ Description

Official website developed for **Maura Balocco**, clinical psychologist, author and researcher in integrated psycho-medicine.

The goal of the project was to create a highly personal and emotionally coherent online presence, able to communicate:

- sensitivity
- authenticity
- human connection
- trust
- intuitive approach

The entire website was built with **Hugo**, using a fully custom structure without relying on traditional CMS platforms.

Special attention was dedicated to:

- visual harmony
- mobile experience
- editorial content structure
- book integration
- social and video presence
- readability and atmosphere

The project required extensive refinement work to balance the artistic identity of the client with modern UX principles and usability.

The final result is a lightweight, elegant and highly customized website designed to represent both the professional and emotional world of the client.
